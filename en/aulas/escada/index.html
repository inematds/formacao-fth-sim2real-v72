Lesson 8 · 13 min

The deployment ladder: sim → sim2sim → suspended → floor

Everything in this course becomes a plan: four steps, each with what runs, the criterion to move up, and the reason to stop and go back. In this lesson you write your own ladder in a YAML file, check it with a script, and simulate the queue of steps with a failure in the middle. The safety procedure with the robot powered on is in course 4.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The four steps and what each lesson in this course delivers to them
  2. 2Pass criterion in the command → expected output format
  3. 3The queue of steps: if one fails, go back a step
  4. 4Your plan with dates

Goal: write the ladder sim → sim2sim → suspended → floor with a pass criterion and a stop criterion at each step, and check it with a script.

03 · Your ladder in YAML

Copy the template to ~/fth/escada.yaml and swap in your robot and your criteria. conferir_escada.py requires the 4 steps in order, with onde, criterio (command → output), and when to stop.

File · kit/escada-modelo.yaml
# escada.yaml — the deployment ladder for your policy (FTH 2, lesson 8)
# Each step: what runs, the criterion to move up (command → expected output) and when to stop and go back.
politica: unitree_rl_lab G1 29 GDL velocity v0 (ONNX)
robo: Unitree G1 EDU (29 GDL)
degraus:
  - nome: sim
    onde: MuJoCo 3.16 + G1 do Menagerie, com as faixas de randomização do treino
    criterio: "python3 kit/randomizar.py --seeds 20 --faixa treino → sobreviveu: 20/20"
    parar_se: qualquer queda dentro da faixa do treino
  - nome: sim2sim
    onde: outro MJCF (unitree_mujoco) e o caminho de mensagens do robô (DDS, rt/lowcmd e rt/lowstate)
    criterio: "python3 kit/portao_sim2sim.py --passo 0.001 --dds → PORTÃO: passou (3 de 3 etapas)"
    parar_se: queda, velocidade fora de ±0,15 m/s, pernas no limite de torque ou política abaixo de 45 Hz
  - nome: suspenso
    onde: robô no pórtico, pés fora do chão, elástico ou cabo de segurança
    criterio: "ensaio de bancada + sysid → kp identificado e atraso medido; a política mexe as pernas sem bater no limite"
    parar_se: junta batendo no limite, vibração, temperatura subindo, atraso maior que o simulado
  - nome: chão
    onde: piso plano, área isolada, cabo frouxo no pórtico, pessoa no botão de parada
    criterio: "teste no chão → 10 s parado + 10 s a 0,3 m/s sem queda, três vezes seguidas"
    parar_se: tropeço, queda, qualquer pessoa dentro da área
Terminal · check the ladder
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/sim2real && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
[ -f $F/escada.yaml ] || cp kit/escada-modelo.yaml $F/escada.yaml
python3 kit/conferir_escada.py $F/escada.yaml; echo "saída: $?"
Terminal · ladder with no stop criterion (common mistake)
F=${FTH:-$HOME/fth}; cd $F/sim2real && . $F/.venv/bin/activate
grep -v "parar_se: tropeço" kit/escada-modelo.yaml > $F/escada-incompleta.yaml
python3 kit/conferir_escada.py $F/escada-incompleta.yaml; echo "saída: $?"

A step with no stop condition is the most expensive mistake: on the floor, it's what tells you when to press the button. The script fails it.
